Design this interface as a professional production product, not an AI-generated template.

Establish a small design system before building pages:
- typography hierarchy
- spacing scale
- content width
- border radius
- borders
- shadows
- colors
- buttons
- inputs
- cards
- navigation
- feedback states

Prefer restrained, timeless visual design over excessive gradients, glassmorphism, animations or decorative elements.

Maintain visual consistency across every page.

Every design decision must support hierarchy, readability and usability.
